About CompCompare

A simple academic tool to find, study, and compare electronic components.

What it does

CompCompare stores a curated SQLite catalog of resistors, capacitors, diodes, LEDs, transistors, inductors, and ICs. You can search by name or part number, filter by category, sort results, open detailed pages, and compare two or three parts side by side.

Who it helps

Built for ECE students, junior engineers, and hobbyists who need a fast way to recall key ratings and choose a sensible part before soldering or simulating a circuit.

Real-world suggestions

Each component includes a practical selection tip — for example, which resistor to pair with a 5 V LED, or when to prefer a MOSFET over a BJT for higher current loads.
